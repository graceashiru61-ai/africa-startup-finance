A financial model is one of the most important tools a startup founder can have.

It helps founders understand their business, plan their growth, and communicate clearly with investors.

## What is a financial model?

A financial model is a structured forecast of a company's future performance.

It usually includes:

- Revenue projections
- Operating expenses
- Hiring plans
- Cash flow forecasts
- Funding requirements
- Growth scenarios

## Why African startups need strong financial models

Startups often face limited resources and uncertain markets.

A good financial model helps founders answer:

- How much money do we need?
- How long will our funding last?
- When will we reach profitability?
- What happens if growth is slower than expected?

## Financial models for fundraising

Investors want to understand the assumptions behind your business.

A strong model shows that you understand:

- Your customers
- Your costs
- Your growth strategy
- Your funding needs

## Final thoughts

A financial model is not just a spreadsheet.

It is a decision-making tool that helps founders build stronger companies.
